# BIG CHRONICLE

**Turn your adventure into an RPG biography — and fill a Pokédex-style bestiary with every monster you meet.**

> *Level 32 — Began my first adventure in Stranglethorn Vale. Here begins the chronicle of ○○, Human Warrior.*
>
> *Level 34 — Arrived in the strange land of Desolace. A new story feels close at hand.*
>
> *Level 34 — Faced the centaur for the first time in Desolace. First entry: "Magram Marauder". (Bestiary No.008)*
>
> *Finished the Maraudon expedition — 1 hr 12 min, 4 bosses defeated, 1 death.*
>
> *Level 40! At last I've earned the right to ride across the continent.*

Just install it and play. Every new zone, level-up, dungeon run and monster encounter adds another line to your own personal chronicle.

> **Note:** the in-game text of BIG CHRONICLE is in **Korean**.

## 📖 Adventure Journal — Your Own RPG Biography

Moments recorded automatically:

- **Exploration** — first visits to zones, and to subzones such as towns and caves
- **Growth** — level-ups (special lines at 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 60) and how long each level took
- **Quests** — completed quests, with experience and gold rewards
- **Dungeon expeditions** — expedition start (party size), first and repeat boss kills, and an end-of-run summary (duration, bosses, deaths, wipes)
- **Battlegrounds** — every battle you join
- **Trials** — where you fell, and the last enemy you were fighting
- **Bestiary moments** — your first meeting with each tribe is written as a short scene from a novel, shaped by the tribe and the time of day
- **Wealth** — your first 1 · 10 · 50 · 100 · 250 · 500 · 1,000 · 5,000 gold
- **Hearthstone** — whenever you set a new home
- **Your own notes** — write a line yourself with `/bc note Rode a gryphon for the first time today`

The journal is divided into **seven chapters** by level:

**Chapter 1 · First Steps** → Chapter 2 · The Adventurer's Road → Chapter 3 · A Widening World → Chapter 4 · Lands of Trial → Chapter 5 · Making a Name → Chapter 6 · Threshold of Legend → **Chapter 7 · Hero of Azeroth**

- Entries are grouped by date and can be filtered by category.
- The **Export** button turns your whole chronicle into plain text you can copy and share.
- Korean grammatical particles are adjusted automatically so every sentence reads naturally.

## 🐾 Monster Bestiary — Collect Them by Tribe

Monsters are registered the moment you **target them, mouse over them, or see their nameplate**.

**34 tribes to discover**
Murloc · Gnoll · Kobold · Trogg · Ogre · Troll · Harpy · Centaur · Quilboar · Furbolg · Satyr · Naga · Yeti & Wendigo · Worgen · Defias Brotherhood · Syndicate · Scarlet Crusade · Venture Co. · Pirates · Dark Iron Dwarves · Hostile Orc Clans · Twilight's Hammer · Grimtotem · Silithid · Dragonkin · Demon · Elemental · Giant · Undead · Mechanical · Beast · Critter · Other Humanoids · Unknown

On top of that, **beast families** such as wolves, spiders and raptors each get their own new bestiary number as you find them.

- **Hidden entries** — undiscovered tribes show only `???` and a one-line hint. Go and find them.
- **Unlockable lore** — every tribe has three paragraphs of lore that unlock one by one as your research rank rises.
  - ★ Encountered → ★★ Observer → ★★★ Researcher → ★★★★ Authority → ★★★★★ Legendary Hunter
  - Research score = species found ×10 + kills + rare creatures ×50 + bosses ×20
- **Habitats** — the zones where you actually met each tribe, how often, plus hints about where else they live
- **Rare collection** — silver-dragon rare spawns are gathered on their own page, marked once you defeat them
- **3D models** — pick a creature to see its model and drag to rotate it
- **Kill titles** — every 50 · 100 · 250 · 500 · 1,000 kills of a tribe earns a title in your journal, such as "Murloc Hunter" or "Bane of the Murlocs"
- **Tooltips** — hovering over a monster shows its bestiary number, tribe and your kill count
- **Smart classification** — creatures are sorted by name and creature type, and the addon learns name prefixes: once it sees "Riverpaw Gnoll", it also recognizes "Riverpaw Mongrel" as a gnoll. If a creature lands in the wrong tribe, right-click it to reassign it.

## 🏆 Achievements — 386 of them

Inspired by the Wrath-era achievements for classic (level 1–60) content, focusing on what **Forever's official achievements don't already cover**, plus achievements unique to this addon:

- **Growth** — levels 10–60, 10 to 5,000 gold, time adventured
- **Exploration** — number of zones and subzones visited, hearthstone changes (per-zone exploration is left to Forever's own achievements)
- **Quests** — 10 to 1,000 quests, quests across many zones or within one zone
- **Dungeons & Raids** — conquer each of the 19 classic dungeons and 7 classic raids, dungeon-count metas, run counts, boss kills, coming back from a wipe, flawless runs
- **Bestiary** — tribes, species, rare creatures, kill counts, research ranks, beast families, 100 kills of iconic tribes
- **Tribe research** — a first meeting, a Researcher rank and 100 kills for every one of the 34 tribes
- **Storytellers** — complete quests in each of the 40 classic zones, then earn the Eastern Kingdoms, Kalimdor and Azeroth titles
- **Battlegrounds, Reputation, Trials, Chronicle** — battles joined, exalted factions, your first death and deathless levels, personal notes and exports
- **Little Joys** — playful achievements based on real-world dates: 7- and 30-day streaks, 100 days together, weekend warrior, night owl, New Year's Day…

Every achievement arrives as a parchment dispatch sealed with red wax and adds a short line of story to your journal.

## ✨ Effects

- The **leather front cover** really swings open on its spine every time you open the book
- Changing chapters or turning journal pages **flips an actual parchment sheet** — forward for later chapters, backward for earlier ones
- Reaching a new chapter unfurls a **dark cinematic band with a gilded chapter title between laurel branches**, with drifting gold dust
- Every notification is **stamped with a red wax seal** — a laurel seal for achievements, a dragon seal for bestiary discoveries (new tribes, new creatures, rares and research ranks)
- The first time you open a newly found tribe, a **dragon wax seal** is pressed onto the page
- Newly earned achievements glow gold for three seconds when you first see them, then keep a quiet NEW tag until you leave the tab
- The **minimap button glows** when something new happened while the window was closed
- Every effect can be turned off in the settings

## 🎨 Leather Notebook UI

A plain, heavy **leather notebook**. No ornaments.

- The cover is a **real leather photo texture** and the pages a **real paper photo texture** (both CC0)
- Flat leather tabs and buttons, thin border lines, clean lists on paper
- Uses the **game's own fonts**
- **Light and dark page** themes
- Lore is revealed with a **typewriter effect** (click or press Space to show it all at once)
- New journal entries gently fade in
- Discovering a new tribe or rare creature brings a **wax-sealed parchment dispatch** to the top of the screen — click it to jump straight to that creature's bestiary page
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: `1–5` switch tabs · `←/→` turn journal pages · `↑/↓` browse the bestiary · `Space` skip the typewriter · `Esc` close (disabled automatically in combat)

## ⌨️ Commands

- `/bc` or `/chronicle` — open or close the window
- `/bc journal` · `/bc dex` · `/bc achievements` · `/bc stats` · `/bc config` — open a specific tab
- `/bc note <text>` — add your own entry to the journal
- `/bc export` — show the whole chronicle as copyable text
- `/bc minimap` — show or hide the minimap button

Minimap button: left-click opens the journal · right-click opens the bestiary · drag to move it

## ❓ FAQ

**Does it record adventures from before I installed it?**
No. The game doesn't expose your past history, so the chronicle starts the moment you install the addon. A level 32 character's first entry will therefore read "Level 32 — Began my first adventure in …".

**Where is my data saved?**
Each character has its own journal and bestiary. Only the settings are shared across your account.

**Some kills aren't counted.**
Kills are counted from two sources — seeing a target or nameplate die, and the experience-gain message — and duplicates are cancelled out. A monster that gives no experience and that you never targeted may be missed.

**Why aren't guards or vendors in the bestiary?**
Faction NPCs, friendly NPCs, totems and pets are left out on purpose.

**The 3D model doesn't show up.**
Models only appear when the game has information about that creature cached. Seeing it up close once usually fixes it.

**The addon list says "Out of date".**
A patch has changed the interface number. Enable "Load out of date AddOns" in the addon list; an update will follow soon.
