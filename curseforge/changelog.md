## 1.3.0

- **Field notebook redesign**: the whole window is now a leather-bound spiral notebook with a stitched cover, grid-paper pages, spiral rings, a bookmark ribbon and colorful index tabs; handwritten pen titles, taped-on panels, highlighter selection, pen checkmarks and a polaroid photo for 3D models
- Themes renamed to **Cream notebook** (light) and **Night notebook** (dark)
- **New wax seals**: rebuilt from a game-icons.net design with embossed, glossy red wax; a laurel seal for achievements and a paw-print seal for new bestiary pages
- **Effects**: the cover flips open when the window opens; chapter titles appear on a torn, taped-on note with a red pen underline; notifications are sticky notes that drop in and stamp a seal with wax crumbs and confetti
- Fixed: newly earned achievements in the Achievements tab kept blinking forever. They now sparkle for three seconds the first time you see them, then keep a quiet NEW tag until you leave the tab
- Credits for the free assets used (Subtle Patterns, game-icons.net, Nanum Pen Script) are in `Media/LICENSES.txt`

## 1.2.0

- **386 achievements** in 12 categories (up from 124): first meeting, Researcher rank and 100 kills for every one of the 34 tribes; a "Storyteller" achievement for quests in each of the 40 classic zones, plus Eastern Kingdoms, Kalimdor and Azeroth metas; tours of both continents; a "regular" achievement for every classic dungeon and raid; more level, gold, quest, kill and bestiary tiers; and a new "Little Joys" category based on real-world dates (7- and 30-day streaks, 100 days, weekend warrior, night owl, New Year's Day and more)
- Achievements that were already completed when a new version adds them are gathered into a single journal line instead of a flood of notifications
- **Effects**: the window unrolls like a scroll when opened; a cinematic chapter title appears in the middle of the screen when you reach a new chapter (and when your chronicle begins); achievement notifications stamp a wax seal and burst with gold sparkles (purple for rares, green for new tribes); a "new record" seal stamps the bestiary page the first time you open a newly found tribe, and the list marks unopened tribes; journal pages sweep like a turning page; newly earned achievements glow; the minimap button glows when something new happened while the window was closed
- New "Effects" option to turn the animations off
- Special thanks to 루마, 절미, 판초네 콘체타, KLEROS

## 1.1.0

- **Achievements**: a new Achievements tab with 10 categories and over a hundred achievements (levels, gold, quests, each classic dungeon and raid, bestiary, battlegrounds, reputation, trials, chronicle), with scroll notifications and a story line in the journal
- **Novel-style bestiary**: first meetings with each tribe are now written as short scenes, shaped by the tribe and the time of day; rare spawns, rank-ups and kill titles were rewritten the same way
- Running the same dungeon twice in a row now counts as two expeditions; leaving to repair or running back as a ghost still counts as one
- Expedition summaries are now placed at the moment you left the dungeon
- "Time since last level" now counts only time spent online
- The death entry no longer names an enemy that was already dead
- Tribe completion and "first encounter" now follow creatures moved to another tribe
- Layout fixes on the bestiary card, creature list and settings tab
- Minimap button and notifications use the new scroll icon
- Clicking a notification opens that creature's bestiary page (tribe, creature and 3D model) or the achievement it announced

## 1.0.0 — First release

- Adventure journal: zone and subzone discoveries, level-ups, quests, dungeon expeditions and boss kills, battlegrounds, deaths, gold milestones, hearthstone changes and personal notes
- Seven level-based chapters, grouping by date, category filters, sort order and plain-text export
- Monster bestiary: 34 tribes plus beast families, unlockable lore (research ranks ★1–5), habitats, rare collection, kill titles, 3D models and tooltips
- Classification that learns name prefixes, plus manual reassignment
- Scroll UI: parchment and dark themes, typewriter lore, keyboard shortcuts, scroll-style notifications and a minimap button
