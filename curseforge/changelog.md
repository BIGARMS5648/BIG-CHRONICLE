## 1.1.0

- **Achievements**: a new Achievements tab with 10 categories and over a hundred achievements (levels, gold, quests, each classic dungeon and raid, bestiary, battlegrounds, reputation, trials, chronicle), with scroll notifications and a story line in the journal
- **Novel-style bestiary**: first meetings with each tribe are now written as short scenes, shaped by the tribe and the time of day; rare spawns, rank-ups and kill titles were rewritten the same way
- Running the same dungeon twice in a row now counts as two expeditions; leaving to repair or running back as a ghost still counts as one
- Expedition summaries are now placed at the moment you left the dungeon
- "Time since last level" now counts only time spent online
- The death entry no longer names an enemy that was already dead
- Tribe completion and "first encounter" now follow creatures moved to another tribe
- Layout fixes on the bestiary card, creature list and settings tab
- Minimap button and notifications use the new scroll icon

## 1.0.0 — First release

- Adventure journal: zone and subzone discoveries, level-ups, quests, dungeon expeditions and boss kills, battlegrounds, deaths, gold milestones, hearthstone changes and personal notes
- Seven level-based chapters, grouping by date, category filters, sort order and plain-text export
- Monster bestiary: 34 tribes plus beast families, unlockable lore (research ranks ★1–5), habitats, rare collection, kill titles, 3D models and tooltips
- Classification that learns name prefixes, plus manual reassignment
- Scroll UI: parchment and dark themes, typewriter lore, keyboard shortcuts, scroll-style notifications and a minimap button
