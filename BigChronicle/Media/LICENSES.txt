BIG CHRONICLE - third-party assets
==================================

The textures in this folder were generated by tools/build_assets.py and
tools/ornate.py from the free assets below. The originals were resized,
recolored and combined with procedurally drawn details (gold relief computed
from height maps, stains, burnt and torn edges, engraved hatching) and saved
as TGA.


1) Parchment and leather textures: Subtle Patterns
   Files used : natural_paper.png, leather_1.png
   Used in    : Parchment.tga, Panel.tga, Leather.tga, and the leather surface
                of Plaque.tga, Strap.tga, Medallion.tga, Tab.tga,
                Button.tga, ButtonActive.tga, ButtonRed.tga
   Source     : https://github.com/atlemo/SubtlePatterns
   License    : Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 (CC BY-SA 3.0)
                https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
   Credit     : Subtle Patterns (subtlepatterns.com), Toptal
   Changes    : resized, made seamless, converted to grayscale for tinting,
                aged with generated stains, cracks and wear. The files listed
                above are shared under the same license.


2) Crest, ornaments, illustrations, icons and wax seal design: game-icons.net
   Icons used : wax-seal, griffin-symbol, dragon-head, laurel-crown,
                fleur-de-lys, compass, curling-vines, curled-leaf,
                stone-bridge, castle-ruins, pine-tree, forest-camp,
                tower-flag, quill-ink, hourglass, cog
   Used in    : Seal.tga, SealDex.tga, SealAch.tga, Emblem.tga, Fleuron.tga,
                Corner.tga, Plaque.tga, Banner.tga, Medallion.tga,
                SketchCastle.tga, SketchCamp.tga, Icons\*.tga
   Source     : https://game-icons.net  (via the @iconify-json/game-icons package,
                https://github.com/game-icons/icons)
   License    : Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)
                https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
   Credit     : Icons by Lorc, Delapouite and the game-icons.net contributors
   Changes    : rasterized; embossed as gold relief or red wax, or redrawn as
                engraved ink sketches (outline plus hatching).
